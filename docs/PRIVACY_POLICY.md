# Vox Reader Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

DRAFT: have this reviewed before you publish it. The Chinese version is [PRIVACY_POLICY.zh-CN.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.zh-CN.md); keep the two in step.

Vox Reader is an offline reader that speaks books and documents aloud. This policy applies to the Huawei AppGallery version.

## What we collect

Nothing. Vox Reader has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. We do not receive your files, your reading history or any information about you.

## What stays on your device

- Your imported books and documents
- Bookmarks, reading position and listening statistics. The weekly free listening allowance is counted from these statistics, on your device.
- Your settings, such as theme, voice and speed

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Removing a book deletes its copy from the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Show the playback controls while the app reads aloud |
| Foreground service (media playback) | Keep reading when the screen is off |
| Wake lock | Stop the phone sleeping in the middle of playback |

The Huawei version does not request internet access at all, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Vox Reader does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage. You choose each file through the system file picker.

**Send feedback** in Settings opens your own email app with the app version, Android version and phone model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and you can edit or delete any of it first.

## Third-party components

| Component | What it does | Data sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sherpa-onnx | Speaks text aloud, using a voice model stored inside the app | None. It runs on the device. |
| Google ML Kit text recognition | Reads text from scanned pages. The recognition model is stored inside the app. | None. It runs on the device. |

If an advertising or purchase service is added in a later version, this policy will be updated first and will name the service, the data it collects and the reason.

## Children

Vox Reader does not knowingly collect any information, from children or anyone else.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete your books and settings at any time from within the app or by uninstalling it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the store page, with a new date at the top.
